## Verbatim copy

**HackMeridian Attendance Support**
Need a way to HackMeridian?
We want great builders in the room.
If a visa, accommodation, or the cost of getting to Lisbon is standing in
your way, you may be eligible for HackMeridian Attendance Support.
Choose the support you need and we'll guide you through the rest.
[Check eligibility](/eligibility)

**Already applied?** Check your application status. Use your last name and
six-character Application Code. This lookup shows only a basic,
non-sensitive status summary. [Check Application Status](/my-application)

**Choose Your Support** — Pick the path that matches what you need.
- **Visa Letter** — I need a visa support letter. Request an official letter
  confirming your participation in HackMeridian to support your visa
  application. [Check Visa Letter Eligibility](/eligibility?supportPath=visa_letter)
- **Accommodation** — I can get to Lisbon, but I need somewhere to stay.
  Approved builders may receive pre-booked hostel accommodation during
  HackMeridian. We'll arrange the booking directly.
  [Check Accommodation Eligibility](/eligibility?supportPath=accommodation)
- **Accommodation + Travel** — I need help getting to Lisbon and somewhere
  to stay. Approved builders may receive pre-booked hostel accommodation and
  travel reimbursement after verification.
  [Check Travel + Stay Eligibility](/eligibility?supportPath=accommodation_travel)

**Important** — Travel support is not paid upfront. If you're approved for
travel support, you will need to purchase your own transportation to
HackMeridian. Before the event, we'll confirm the maximum amount you're
eligible to receive back. Travel approvals use one of four USD tiers: $150,
$350, $600, or $1,000. The tier is assigned based on travel geography,
demonstrated need, and available support. The conversion rate is fixed on
the decision date. Eligible costs are transportation only; after
HackMeridian, payment is made in XLM by default once participation and
documentation requirements are complete.

**Example** — Approved up to $350 and eligible expenses total $200 →
reimbursement is $200 at the decision-date rate. Expenses total $450 →
maximum stays $350 at that same fixed rate.

**Travel Grant Tiers** — Four clear approval levels; maximum USD approvals
for transportation only.
- Tier 1 · Local — $150 — Portugal / nearby ground travel
- Tier 2 · Regional — $350 — Short-haul European travel
- Tier 3 · Extended — $600 — Longer European / nearby international travel
- Tier 4 · Long-Haul — $1,000 — Intercontinental travel
XLM is the default payout asset; USDC only with treasury approval. Rate
fixed on decision date.

**How Attendance Support Works**
1. Tell us what you need (visa letter / accommodation / accommodation+travel).
2. Submit your application (about you, what you plan to build, support needed).
3. Reviewed in waves; applying earlier is encouraged, support is limited.
4. Decision: USD tier by travel geography, need, available support;
   conversion rate fixed on decision date.
5. Come build at HackMeridian: check in, participate, submit your project.
6. Submit project and proof: upload eligible transportation receipts after
   the event.
7. Reimbursement after verification, in XLM by default (USDC only with
   treasury approval).

**Review Waves** — Wave 1 Aug 31 (30 places) · Wave 2 Sep 15 (45) ·
Wave 3 Sep 30 (50) · Wave 4 Oct 7 (45) · Wave 5 Oct 14, final (30).
Total planned: 200 travel-support places — planning numbers, not
guarantees; adjustable by the review team. Applications after the final
wave are not considered for travel or accommodation support.

**FAQ headings:** Is this a project grant? · How much travel support can I
receive? · Which asset will I receive? · Can you pay for my flight before
HackMeridian? · Do I need to already have a project? · How does
accommodation work? · Is travel support guaranteed if I apply?

**Closing** — We want great builders in the room. Tell us what's standing
between you and HackMeridian. We'll tell you if we can help.
[Check Eligibility](/eligibility)
