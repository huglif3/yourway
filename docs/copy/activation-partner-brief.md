Guava Global × Stellar Development Foundation | Everything a partner needs to plan a successful activation.

YOUR ACTIVATION IS A ROUTE.
Every activation runs under HackMeridian's lead-up campaign, FIND YOUR WAY. there is no single, prescribed path into HackMeridian. Your event is one legitimate entry point among many (a workshop, a founder night, a mini-hack), not a checkpoint on a fixed roadshow.
Frame your activation as a route builders can choose, e.g. "YOUR WAY 01/ Vietnam" or "YOUR WAY 01 / Lisbon", and close on the shared CTA: FIND YOUR WAY → HACKMERIDIAN.

Map your activation to one campaign beat:
FIND YOUR PEOPLE (community/founder events)
FIND YOUR STACK (technical workshops)
FIND YOUR IDEA (ideation)
FIND YOUR TEAM (matchmaking/hack nights)
FIND YOUR EDGE (advanced/mentorship)
FIND YOUR NEXT MOVE (mini-hacks & registration).

## 01 THE OPPORTUNITY

HackMeridian Lisbon runs October 25–27, 2026, directly before Meridian, Stellar Development Foundation's flagship conference (Oct 28–29). Builders who travel for HackMeridian can attend Meridian for $49: two days beside SocGen, Amundi, DTCC and MoneyGram-level institutional participation. Two tracks:

|  | GENESIS | SCALE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who | First-time founders & emerging builders validating a new idea or early prototype | Experienced founders/product teams building production-ready solutions |
| Prize | Prize pool ($30k+) + travel grants | Cash Prizes TBD — SCF pathway (up to $150k), platform credibility, direct SDF/ecosystem access |

Programme goal: 250+ builders from Europe & APAC arrive in Lisbon with a project idea, a team, and hands-on Stellar experience — driven through regional activations Sept–Oct 2026.

## 02 THE DELEGATED MODEL

You own the activation in your market. Guava owns the infrastructure behind it. One partner, one market, one focused activation.

| YOU PROVIDE | GUAVA PROVIDES |
| --- | --- |
| • Community access & targeted recruitment • Venue & event execution • Local promotion & personal follow-up • Prizes (if running a mini-hack) • Facilitator, sourced locally so delivery is in your market's native language (fee paid from your budget) | • Content library (workshops, slides, tutorials) for your facilitator to deliver • Funnel tracking links, QR codes, registration materials • Travel grant allocation & disbursement (separate pool, you do not distribute grants) |

Your budget covers: community engagement & outreach, facilitation fees, and prizes (if applicable). Travel grants are managed and paid out by Guava, based on demo-day performance, not something you run yourselves.

## 03 BEFORE YOU DESIGN ANYTHING — THREE NON-NEGOTIABLES

— Qualification moment — something that separates serious builders from curious observers: a project pitch, a scoped-idea form, a 3-minute team standup, a prototype demo.
— Conversion moment — a specific, tracked ask to register for HackMeridian, live in the room: a QR code on screen, a link in chat, a verbal ask with a deadline.
— 48-hour follow-up — every attendee receives the registration link, travel grant info, and next steps within 48 hours. Most conversions happen here, not on the day.

## 05 CONTENT & WORKSHOPS — USE THE LIBRARY, DON'T BUILD FROM SCRATCH

Every technical workshop follows Build (20min) → Demo (10min) → Apply (20min) → Q&A (10min).
Run these at every activation:
— What is Stellar and Why Now — institutional credibility briefing (SocGen, Amundi, Spiko, DTCC, EURC). Opening session, all builders.
— Find Your Stellar Use Case — matches a builder's problem to a Stellar track; run before team formation.
— Build with AI and Soroban — using Claude/Cursor/Copilot to build properly on Soroban. Highest-priority module, all technical builders.
— Beyond HackMeridian — the closing session. SCF pathway, CV Labs EMEA Accelerator, Ambassador Program, told through past winner stories (4Bridge, Stellar Forge, The Simple Fund).
Full menu (Zero to Soroban, Rust for Web2 Developers, Validating Your Idea in 48 Hours, Landing Page with AI, Pitch Like a Founder, and more) is in the Activation Partner Playbook — most modules are available live or as async video for virtual delivery.

## 07 THE PROPOSAL — WHAT WE NEED TO APPROVE YOUR PARTNERSHIP

To confirm a partnership, your proposal must answer the following clearly and specifically — vague or generic answers are returned for revision, not approved. This is what we score:

1. Who exactly is your target community? Name the specific group, not a generic category ("fintech engineers from the neobanking sector," not "developers in London").
2. Which track are you targeting — Genesis, Scale, or both — and why does your community map to it?
3. What format are you running (Mini-Hack Weekend, IRL Event Series, Scale Founder Evening) and why is it right for this audience?
4. What is your qualification moment — the specific thing that separates serious builders from curious observers?
5. What is your conversion moment — the specific, tracked ask for HackMeridian registration, and when does it happen?
6. Which content modules from Guava's library will you use, and who is your local, native-language facilitator?
7. What are your specific KPI targets — attendance, qualified builders, registrations, applications?
8. What are your specific dates, and your budget breakdown (community engagement, facilitation, venue, F&B, prizes)?

Outcomes:
Approved: all questions answered with specific, credible detail.
Returned for revision: audience, conversion plan, or format isn't specific enough.
Declined: partner can't demonstrate reach to the target builder profile, or no KPI targets are set.
