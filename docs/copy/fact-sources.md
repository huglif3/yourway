## Open conflicts (surfaced, not silently resolved)

- **$49 vs the master copy doc.** The master copy doc (source of truth #2)
  says only "discounted Developer tickets", no price; the Brief says $49 and
  ruling D4 chose the Brief's wording. Outstanding for the docs' owners:
  align the master copy doc with the Brief, or overrule D4.
- **"Travel grants" vs "Attendance Support".** The team ruled earlier that
  the programme is named Attendance Support; the V2 Playbook (newer,
  team-authored) says "travel grants" throughout. The partner page follows
  the Playbook's term for the partner-facing pool. The two descriptions are
  reconcilable — demo-day performance decides the shortlist, geography+need
  decides the tier, payment is reimbursement after verified participation —
  and the page states it exactly that way. The naming split needs one ruling
  before the grants landing page ships.
- **"October 25–27" span.** The Brief folds the optional Oct 27 learning day
  into the hackathon range; the schedule ruling keeps the hackathon proper at
  Oct 25–26. **Both pages follow the schedule ruling and say Oct 25–26** —
  the Brief's wider span is not an authority to change them (cto review,
  round 2). The Oct 27 day appears separately where the schedule is listed.
