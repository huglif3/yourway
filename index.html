Questions, then your move.

Who can attend an activation?

Builders, founders, students, and ecosystem-curious participants who want a practical path into building on Stellar.

Who can host an activation?

Community leads, campus groups, founder networks, and local operators with the ability to convene builders and report outcomes.

What support do partners receive?

Partners can receive branding, curriculum, co-marketing, technical mentor support, and guidance on how the activation connects to HackMeridian.

Do I need prior Stellar experience?

No. Some sessions are designed to onboard builders to Stellar, while later rounds focus on moving ideas closer to shipping.

Is this the travel grant application page?

No. Travel support will have a separate application flow later. This page is for activations, event discovery, and host applications.
