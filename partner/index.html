02 · The delegated model

You run the activation. We run the infrastructure.

One partner, one market, multiple focused activations.

You provide

  • Community access and targeted recruitment
  • Venue and event execution
  • Local promotion and personal follow-up
  • Prizes, if you are running a mini-hack
  • A facilitator sourced locally, so delivery is in your community's native language

Guava provides

  • Content library — workshops, slides, tutorials — for your facilitator to deliver
  • Funnel tracking links, QR codes, registration materials
  • Travel grant allocation and disbursement

Your budget covers community engagement and outreach, facilitation fees, and prizes if applicable. Individual activation budgets range $500–$12,000, depending on project scope, number of events, and KPI targets. Payouts are handled by grant application and reimbursement — not a lump sum upfront.

Travel grants are a separate pool

Held and disbursed by Guava — never distributed by partners and never folded into activation budgets. Builders are shortlisted on what they actually build and show at your demo day; you submit that shortlist in your post-event report. The review team then assigns one tier per builder based on travel geography and need.

  • $150Local — Portugal and nearby ground travel
  • $350Regional — short-haul European travel
  • $600Extended — longer European and nearby international
  • $1,000Long-haul — intercontinental travel

03 · The ground rules

Three non-negotiables.

01

Qualification moment

Something that separates serious builders from curious observers: a project pitch, a scoped-idea form, a three-minute team standup, a prototype demo.

02

Conversion moment

A specific, tracked ask to register for HackMeridian, live in the room: a QR code on screen, a link in chat, a verbal ask with a deadline.

03

48-hour follow-up

Every attendee receives the registration link, travel grant info, and next steps within 48 hours. Most conversions happen here, not on the day.

06 · Measuring success

What we track, and what counts.

Activation metrics

  • Reach — community notified
  • Registrations — target 2× attendance
  • Attendance on the day
  • New-to-Stellar builders

HackMeridian conversion

  • HackMeridian registrations via tracked link
  • Applications submitted
  • Confirmed Lisbon attendees
  • Teams formed — 2+ people, scoped project

A qualified builder meets 4+

  • Has a specific problem to solve
  • Relevant domain or technical capability
  • Identified where Stellar adds value
  • Has or is forming a team
  • Registered with a track selected

Minimum threshold. 20% of attendees convert to HackMeridian registrations.

Strong performance. 35%+ conversion earns priority in the next cycle, including travel-grant increases.

Note. Travel grants are shortlisted on demo-day performance, tiered by travel geography and need, and paid after verified participation — never on registration counts.

07 · The proposal

Submit your activation proposal.

Your proposal must answer eight questions clearly and specifically. Vague or generic answers are returned for revision, not approved. Deadline: Friday, September 4, 2026.

  • Approved — every question answered with specific, credible detail.
  • Returned for revision — audience, conversion plan, or format is not specific enough.
  • Declined — no demonstrable reach to the target builder profile, or no KPI targets set.

Reviewed within 5 business days.

  1. Who exactly is your target community? Name the specific group, not a generic category.
  2. Which track are you targeting — Genesis, Scale, or both — and why does your community map to it?
  3. What format are you running, and why is it right for this audience?
  4. What is your qualification moment?
  5. What is your conversion moment, and when does it happen?
  6. Which content modules from Guava's library will you use, and who is your local facilitator?
  7. What are your specific KPI targets — attendance, qualified builders, registrations, applications?
  8. What are your specific dates, and your budget breakdown?

Applications are collected by Grow With Guava LLC (data controller; the company behind the Guava Global campaign name) to assess activation partnerships, and are stored in Notion, our processor. Questions or withdrawal: meridian@growwithguava.com. If you name a facilitator, make sure they know you are sharing their details with us. See our privacy notice for details.
