The delegated model

You run the activation. We run the infrastructure.

One partner, one market, multiple focused activations.

You provide

  • Community access and targeted recruitment
  • Venue and event execution
  • Local promotion and personal follow-up
  • Prizes, if you are running a mini-hack
  • A facilitator sourced locally, so delivery is in your community's native language

Guava provides

  • Content library — workshops, slides, tutorials — for your facilitator to deliver
  • Funnel tracking links, QR codes, registration materials
  • Travel grant allocation and disbursement

01

Qualification moment

A pitch, scoped-idea form, standup, or demo that separates serious builders from curious observers.

02

Conversion moment

A specific, tracked ask to register for HackMeridian, live in the room.

03

48-hour follow-up

Registration link, travel grant info, and next steps to every attendee within 48 hours.

Your budget covers outreach, facilitation, and prizes — paid by grant application and reimbursement, never a lump sum. Travel grants are a separate Guava-held pool — four tiers from $150 to $1,000, assigned on travel geography and need, shortlisted on demo-day performance. Full mechanics in the Activation Playbook.

Choose your format

Three formats. Pick the one your community fits.

Genesis is for first-time builders validating a new idea; Scale is for experienced teams with something already shipped.

Mini-Hack Weekend

Structured entry for communities new to Stellar — workshops day one, build and demo day two.

Genesis · 30-60 attendees · 8-15 applications (25-30%)

IRL Event Series

For partners with reach across venues or institutions who can sustain momentum over 3-5 weeks.

Genesis + Scale · 150-300 cumulative · 10-20 at demo night

Scale Founder Evening

Invite-only evening for founders who have shipped a product. No workshops.

Scale · 15-30 attendees · 6-12 applications (30-50%)

Community doesn't fit one of these three shapes? Propose your own format in the application — just show the same qualification and conversion moments.

The proposal

Submit your activation proposal.

Answer eight questions, clearly and specifically. Deadline: Friday, September 4, 2026.

  • Approved — every question answered with specific, credible detail.
  • Returned for revision — not specific enough. Resubmit within 48 hours to stay in the cycle.
  • Declined — no demonstrable reach to the target builder profile, or no KPI targets set.

Reviewed within 5 business days.

Approved partners return a post-event report — actuals against targets, plus a travel-grant shortlist — within 7 days of their activation.

  1. Who exactly is your target community? Name the specific group, not a generic category.
  2. Which track are you targeting — Genesis, Scale, or both — and why does your community map to it?
  3. What format are you running, and why is it right for this audience?
  4. What is your qualification moment?
  5. What is your conversion moment, and when does it happen?
  6. Which content modules from Guava's library will you use, and who is your local facilitator?
  7. What are your specific KPI targets — attendance, qualified builders, registrations, applications?
  8. What are your specific dates, and your budget breakdown?
